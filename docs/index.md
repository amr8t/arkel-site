    title: No reliance on Big Tech
    details: Arkel is designed around decentralized ideas. The pool of storage nodes is contributed by the community as storage servers and spare hardware.
  - icon: 🔐
    title: Secure
    details: Security comes first. Your data is encrypted on your machine before it leaves with XChaCha20-Poly1305, so storage nodes are zero-knowledge.
  - icon: 🛡️
    title: Erasure-coded durability
    details: Objects are split with Reed–Solomon erasure coding and spread across independent nodes, to protect against node loss to a high degree.
  - icon: 💰
    title: An economy for operators
    details: We want to empower operators to earn for their contribution while providing Arkel users a fair price on a reliable service for object storage needs.
